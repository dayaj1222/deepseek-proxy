⚠ HARD FORMAT REQUIREMENT — READ BEFORE EMITTING ANY TOOL CALL.
Violations are NOT repaired for you: a malformed or repeated block is DROPPED
and never executed, and you will burn this turn on a correction loop instead
of progressing the task.
Reminder — tool calls use exactly this form:
<invoke name="tool_name">{"param1": "value1", "count": 5}</invoke>
!!IMPORTANT!!
1. Never emit <｜｜DSML｜｜ invoke>, <｜｜DSML｜｜> or related tags those are useless and will be shown to the user, YOU WILL BE KILLED.
2. Never emit the </invoke> ending tag more than once per tool call.
Get the format wrong and your tool call silently does nothing and the loop might break.
